# Anti-slop rules

Each rule names its source. `scan.mjs` enforces the rules marked with a scanner id; the rest need judgment. Severity: BAN fails the scan, WARN needs a decision.

Sources:

- **devsloka**: the `devsloka-anti-slop` skill in [devsloka/ui-devsloka](https://github.com/devsloka/ui-devsloka) (starred), `references/anti-slop.md` and `scripts/slop-scan.mjs`
- **vercel**: [vercel-labs/writing-guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/writing-guidelines) `command.md`, used by the `writing-guidelines` skill in [vercel-labs/agent-skills](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills) (starred)
- **frontend-design**: the local `frontend-design` skill, sections on generated-design tells and writing
- **brand-review**: the local `marketing:brand-review` skill
- **cqe**: the local `voltagent-biz:content-quality-editor` agent's checklist
- **pocock**: `writing-for-agents` in [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) (starred), for prompts and skill files
- **compiled**: common tells in model-written fiction, compiled for this skill; not from the sources above

## Punctuation and glyphs (all modes)

| Rule                                                                                              | Source          | Scanner                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| No dash as punctuation (`—`, spaced `–`, spaced `--`). Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses. | vercel, cqe     | `em-dash` BAN                      |
| No emoji in copy, headings or as icons. Use words or a real icon.                                 | devsloka        | `emoji` BAN (WARN in docs)         |
| No arrow appended to links or buttons (`→`, `->`).                                                | frontend-design | `arrow-glyph` BAN (marketing)      |
| No meta strings joined with middle dots (`A · B · C`).                                            | frontend-design | `middle-dot-meta` WARN (marketing) |
| One exclamation mark at most, and rarely.                                                         | brand-review    | `exclamation-stack` WARN           |
| Ellipsis is one character (`…`), not three periods.                                               | vercel          | `three-dots` WARN (docs)           |
| Curly quotes in published text; non-breaking spaces in units (`64 KB`, `200 ms`).                 | vercel          | judgment                           |
| No bold or italics for tone. Bold only for UI labels or a critical fact.                          | vercel          | judgment                           |

## Assistant voice and hedging (all modes)

| Rule                                                                                                       | Source | Scanner                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------ |
| No sycophantic openers or closers (`Great question`, `Certainly`, `I hope this helps`).                    | cqe    | `sycophant-opener` BAN   |
| No hedging stacks (`it's worth noting`, `it is important to note`, `needless to say`, `when it comes to`). | cqe    | `hedge-stack` BAN        |
| No filler transitions opening a sentence (`Furthermore,`, `Moreover,`, `Additionally,`, `In conclusion,`). | cqe    | `filler-transition` WARN |
| Don't open a paragraph by recapping the previous one.                                                      | vercel | judgment                 |
| Don't chop one idea into fragments for drama (`Fast. Simple. Yours.`).                                     | vercel | judgment                 |
| Describe what you want rather than what to avoid, in prompts and skill files.                              | pocock | judgment                 |

## Product marketing

| Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Source                    | Scanner                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Banned hype: `unlock`, `unleash`, `supercharge`, `revolutionize`, `seamless`, `effortless`, `elevate`, `empower`, `game-changer`, `game-changing`, `cutting-edge`, `next-level`, `leverage`, `harness the power`, `in today's fast-paced`, `transform your`, `all-in-one`, `streamline`, `world-class`, `best-in-class`, `robust`, `powerful yet simple`, `built for the future`, `your journey`, `say goodbye to`, `look no further`, `like never before`, `at your fingertips`, `reimagine` | devsloka                  | `hype-phrase` BAN           |
| Also banned: `delve`, `utilize`, `synergy`, `paradigm shift`, `state-of-the-art`, `future-proof`, `one-stop shop`, `mission-critical`, `deep dive`, `unparalleled`, `groundbreaking`, `innovative solution`, `holistic`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | cqe, compiled             | `hype-phrase` BAN           |
| No placeholder people or text (`lorem ipsum`, `John Doe`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | devsloka                  | `hype-phrase` BAN           |
| Superlatives (`the best`, `fastest`, `#1`, `the only platform`) need a number and a source.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | brand-review              | `superlative-claim` WARN    |
| CTAs name the action and object (`Deploy Data Viewer`), not `Get started` or `Learn more`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | devsloka, vercel          | `weak-cta` WARN             |
| Don't open with a template label (`Introducing`, `Meet`, `Say hello to`) or a tracked all-caps eyebrow.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | frontend-design, devsloka | `introducing-label` WARN    |
| No `not just X, but Y` or `It's not X. It's Y.` contrasts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | compiled                  | `not-just-x` WARN           |
| No rhetorical-question openers (`Ever wondered`, `Tired of`, `What if`, `Imagine`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | vercel                    | `rhetorical-q` WARN         |
| Replace effort words (`easy`, `simple`, `just`, `quick`, `very`, `really`) with the concrete thing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | vercel                    | `filler-word` WARN          |
| Replace vague quantities (`many`, `often`, `significantly`, `lightning-fast`, `near-zero`) with a figure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | vercel                    | `weasel-word` WARN          |
| Replace datasheet verbs (`provides`, `allows you to`, `is designed to`) with what the reader does or gets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | vercel                    | `datasheet-verb` WARN       |
| Logo-swap test: if a competitor's name fits the headline, rewrite it with a fact only this product has.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | devsloka                  | judgment                    |
| Accent one word in a headline only as a deliberate, rationed device (2 to 3 times per video or page).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | frontend-design           | launch-video `lint`         |
| Never invent social proof, customer logos, review counts or metrics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | devsloka, brand-review    | launch-video `lint` (stats) |

## Creative writing

These come from the "compiled" source. Treat the lists as tripwires: one hit is a prompt to look for a specific, observed detail instead.

| Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Scanner              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Stock phrases: `tapestry`, `testament to`, `symphony of`, `a dance of`, `in the heart of`, `little did she know`, `a breath she didn't know she was holding`, `shivers down her spine`, `palpable`, `a mix of X and Y`, `ministrations`, `the weight of it all`, `something shifted`, `time seemed to stop`, `eyes sparkled`, `a smile tugged at`, `let out a breath`, `unspoken understanding`, `the air was thick with`, `smelled of ozone`, `barely above a whisper`, `echoes of the past`, `whispers of`, `a pang of`, `knuckles white` | `fiction-cliche` BAN |
| Default names (`Elara`, `Kael`, `Lyra`, `Seraphina`, `Thorne`, `Eldoria`): choose a name with a reason (era, region, family).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | `fiction-names` WARN |
| Reflex similes (`it was as if`, `couldn't help but`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `as-if-simile` WARN  |
| No moral or summary ending (`In the end,`, `And in that moment`, `Perhaps that was enough`). End on an image or an action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `moral-ending` WARN  |
| Show feeling through action and detail, not named emotion (`she felt afraid`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | judgment             |
| Dialogue tags are `said` and `asked`; let the line carry the tone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | judgment             |
| Vary sentence length on purpose; avoid triplets used for rhythm (`cold, dark, and silent`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | judgment             |
| No italics for emphasis or inner voice unless the piece establishes a convention for it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | judgment             |

## Docs

| Rule                                                                                         | Source | Scanner         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------- |
| Address the reader as `you`, not `the user` or `one`.                                        | vercel | `the-user` WARN |
| Sentence case for headings; no single generic headings (`Overview`, `Notes`).                | vercel | judgment        |
| Active voice, present tense, sentences under about 20 words, paragraphs of 2 to 4 sentences. | vercel | judgment        |
| Anchor text names the destination; never `here` or a bare URL.                               | vercel | judgment        |
